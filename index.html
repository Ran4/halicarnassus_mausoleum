The Mausoleum at HalicarnassusCaria · c. 350 BC

Μαυσωλεῖον τῆς Ἁλικαρνασσοῦ

The tomb of Mausolus and Artemisia as it stood new: a three-tiered podium bearing hundreds of painted marble statues, thirty-six Ionic columns, a pyramid of twenty-four steps and the marble quadriga forty-five metres above the terrace. Around it the walled precinct, and below it the city of Halicarnassus running down to the harbour.
